## How this is calculated

- **Module complete**: at least 3,000 words of written content. Below that it's a **skeleton** (files exist, the module was never written).
- **Not started**: in the curriculum plan, not on disk yet.
- **Exercise solved**: its solution has real content, not a scaffold stub.
- **Learning %** = complete modules / all modules. **Solutions %** = solved exercises / all exercises. **Overall** = the mean of the two (learning only where there's no solutions repo).
- Skeletons and stubs are queued for the content pipeline automatically, entry-level roles first.
