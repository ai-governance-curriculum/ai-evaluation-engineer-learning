# mod-111-gpai-systemic-risk-assurance: GenAI / GPAI Systemic-Risk Assurance

Reads the *frontier-model* half of release-assurance — the surface where the release-assurance methodology owner is discharging obligations attached not to a specific downstream product but to the general-purpose AI *model* upstream of any product. Chapters walk the statutory driver (EU AI Act Article 55, layered on the Article 51 / 52 classification path, discharged operationally through the [EU General-Purpose AI Code of Practice](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/ai-code-practice)), the industry-shape template (Anthropic RSP, OpenAI Preparedness, Google DeepMind FSF, Meta Frontier AI Framework, and the Frontier Model Forum's shared library), the evaluator-facing envelope the UK and US AI Safety Institutes attach to (the AI 600-1 GenAI Profile of the NIST AI RMF as *what* is evaluated; TEVV as *how* it is evaluated), the safety-benchmark citation pack that discharges "state-of-the-art model evaluation" under Article 55(1)(a) — with methodology depth owned by the peer `model-evaluation-engineer`, `ai-risk-engineer`, and `agentic-safety-engineer` roles — and the content-provenance-and-openness overlay (C2PA for Article 50 machine-readable marking; the NTIA dual-use foundation-model report for the openness-position analysis). Closed by the *systemic-risk assurance section* of the GPAI system card the European AI Office (and equivalent bodies) will accept.

**Estimated effort:** 14 hours

## Learning objectives

- Read EU AI Act Article 55 systemic-risk GPAI obligations and the EU GPAI Code of Practice as the reference shape for GPAI evaluation duties
- Compare frontier-lab public deployment-tier frameworks (Anthropic RSP, OpenAI Preparedness, Google DeepMind FSF, Frontier Model Forum publications) as the industry shape template
- Read UK AISI and US AISI public evaluation guidance and the NIST TEVV framing for GenAI (AI RMF Generative AI Profile) as the third-party-evaluator-facing shape
- Reason about MLCommons AI Safety Working Group benchmarks, AIR-Bench 2024, HarmBench, AgentDojo, InjecAgent as evidence types the assurance package cites — with depth owned by `model-evaluation-engineer` and `ai-risk-engineer`
- Reason about frontier-agent red-team evidence from `agentic-safety-engineer` (level 40) and consume it into the assurance package without duplicating the methodology depth
- Reason about C2PA content provenance for GenAI outputs and NTIA dual-use foundation model report obligations
- Author the systemic-risk-assurance section of a GPAI system card that the European AI Office (and equivalent bodies) will accept

## Suggested pace

- **Chapter `01`** — read once, then read Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 Articles 3(65), 51, 52, 53, 55, and 56 in the consolidated text at [eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/1689/oj](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/1689/oj), plus Annex XIII, plus the current version of the [EU GPAI Code of Practice](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/ai-code-practice). Exercise `01` builds the Article 55 + Code of Practice obligation map for a worked GPAI-systemic-risk release.
- **Chapter `02`** — read alongside the four current published frontier frameworks and at least two [Frontier Model Forum](https://www.frontiermodelforum.org/) issue briefs. Exercise `02` builds the comparative-read table.
- **Chapter `03`** — read alongside [NIST AI 600-1](https://doi.org/10.6028/NIST.AI.600-1) end-to-end, the [UK AISI](https://www.aisi.gov.uk/) publications page, the [Inspect](https://inspect.aisi.org.uk/) documentation, and the [US AISI](https://www.nist.gov/aisi) landing page. Exercise `03` designs the evaluator envelope for a specific systemic-risk GPAI.
- **Chapter `04`** — read alongside the arXiv preprints for each benchmark named in the chapter and the [MLCommons AI Safety Working Group](https://mlcommons.org/ai-safety/) landing page. Exercise `04` builds the safety-benchmark citation pack for a release candidate.
- **Chapter `05`** — read alongside the current [C2PA technical specification](https://c2pa.org/specifications/specifications/) and the [NTIA report](https://www.ntia.gov/programs-and-initiatives/artificial-intelligence/report-on-dual-use-foundation-models) end-to-end. Exercise `05` overlays the C2PA-provenance and NTIA-openness sections onto a specific GPAI-systemic-risk assurance package.
